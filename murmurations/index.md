This was the webpage for the number theory study group on **Murmurations in
Arithmetic**, run in Term 2 of 2023/24 at Warwick and organised by
[Sam Chow](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/people/staff/chow/),
[Simon Rydin Myerson](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/people/staff/myerson/)
and Robin Visser. It met on Fridays, 1–2pm, in B3.03 and on MS Teams.
