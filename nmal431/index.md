Welcome to the **Advanced Algebraic Number Theory NMAL431** course webpage for
the academic year 2026/27.

**When and where.** Every **Thursday** at **12:20** for 90 minutes, and again at
**14:00** for 90 minutes, in room **K10C** in Karlín. Every second week, starting
9 October 2025, the 14:00 session is an exercise class.

This course continues *Basic Algebraic Number Theory NMAG472*
([notes]({{ site.nmal431_base }}/bant.pdf)). It broadly follows Jack Thorne's
algebraic number theory lecture notes
([pdf]({{ site.nmal431_base }}/jackthorne_notes.pdf)), though the lectures also
cover some topics and theorems not in those notes. The aims are to cover:

- Dedekind domains, localisations, and completion at a prime.
- Galois theory of local and global fields.
- An introduction to global class field theory.

## Assessment

The assessment is an oral exam, in which you will be asked to prove a theorem
from the course. If you would like to take the course for credit, please
[email me](mailto:robin.visser@matfyz.cuni.cz) with a proposed exam date and
time. You may sit the exam on any day up until 20 September 2026.
